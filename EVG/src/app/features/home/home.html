EVG provides a comprehensive digital ecosystem that supports all vehicle stakeholders across the UAE.

Since 2015, Emirates Vehicle Gate (EVG) has played a key role in transforming the UAE's vehicle services landscape. Designed as an integrated digital ecosystem, EVG connects all major stakeholders — individual drivers, insurance companies, banks, brokers, workshops, and fleet operators — through a unified platform that simplifies, digitizes, and secures every aspect of vehicle ownership and operations.

With smart features, verified data, and end-to-end service workflows, EVG continues to support the UAE's vision of efficient, connected, and technology-driven mobility.
